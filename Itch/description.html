lowercase / UPPERCASE

You are the missing letter. Every level is a sentence with one blank in it, and the world is built from that sentence: words are the floors, punctuation is the machinery, and a single yellow letter has to find its way into the gap.

Hold Shift and you become A: big, heavy and slow, strong enough to smash cracked words. Let go and you are a again: small, light and fast, able to slip under low words and fly off springs. One key, two letters, and every obstacle asks the same question: which form, and when?

Two forms, one key

  • a (Shift released): runs fast and jumps high. Fits under low words, hangs and swings from commas, crosses hyphen bridges, drops through the holes between kerned letters, and flies twice as high off a spring.
  • A (Shift held): slow, with barely a hop, but heavy. Smashes cracked words, holds periods down to keep doors open, stomps enemies from above, and walks straight over kerning holes.

Growing needs headroom: under a low ceiling, Shift waits until there is room. Shrinking in mid-air keeps your speed, so a quick A-drop into a release turns into a long glide.

A world made of text

  • brick is a cracked wall. Only A breaks it.
  • lowlowlow stacked over the floor is a crawl space. Only a fits.
  • . is a pressure plate. Stand on it as A and its door opens.
  • -------- is a bridge that snaps under A.
  • ! is a spring, , is a hook to swing from, and letters set far apart (g a p) have holes a can fall through.
  • Words do what they say: ice slides, hot burns if you stay, up is a lift, sticky slows you down but lets you climb its walls.
  • LOCK zones turn on Caps Lock: you are A unless you hold Shift, until you find the key.
  • Hit a [*] power block from below to get asterisks to shoot.
  • Collect #, pass ; checkpoints, and finish by slotting into the blank: hide in a cr_ck.

Enemies

Anything red or orange bites, and touching it costs a life. Some die when A lands on them, some only to asterisks, and a few can't be stopped at all: you just have to get past.

  • & Walker: paces along its word. Stomp it as A or shoot it.
  • § Armour: asterisks bounce off it. Only a capital landing on it gets through.
  • ^ Spike: walks like the others, but landing on it hurts. Shoot it.
  • ! Hopper: hops on a beat. Pass under it while it is up.
  • ~ Flier: bobs and drifts through the air over gaps and bridges.
  • « < Arrow: fired from a launcher. Flies over a and hits A.
  • ¡ Riser: rises out of a hole in a spaced-out word, and stays down while you stand by it.
  • ' Drip: hangs under a ceiling and falls when you pass below. It grows back.
  • ° Spark: leaps out of a hot word and dives back in. Time your way past.
  • o···· Fire bar: a bar of fire turning around a hot o. a slips under it.
  • @ Curl: land on it as A and it curls into an o. Touch the o to kick it into other enemies.
  • ö Eye: wakes when you come near and drifts after you, even through words. Shoot it.
  • ↕ Case swapper: touch it and Shift works the other way until you touch another.
  • b Mimic: copies your case a moment late. Turn A just before you land on the small b.
  • | Cursor: chases you and erases the words behind you. Keep moving.

The run

  • 15 levels in 5 chapters. The first two levels of each chapter teach something new; the third tests it and ends in a run that makes you switch faster and faster.
  • 60 seconds per level, three lives per run. Run out of time and the run is over.
  • Score for enemies, # collectibles and every second left on the clock, with a top-10 high score table.
  • Random mode: endless levels stitched together from hand-made sections, just for practice and fun (no high scores).
  • Two sound sets to choose from: Chip and Type.

Controls

  • Move: ← → or A / D (left stick)
  • Jump: Space, ↑ or W (A / Cross)
  • Grow: hold Shift (either trigger)
  • Shoot: Ctrl, after hitting a power block (X / Square). In the browser, use F instead: Ctrl+W closes the tab.
  • Restart level: R  ·  Pause: Esc (Start)

Made for GitHub Game Off 2026: SHIFT

The theme is the controller. The whole game is played on the Shift key, and its only verb is the one the key was named for: on a typewriter, pressing Shift physically shifted the type into position to print the capital. Here it shifts a letter between its two cases, and with it everything that matters: size, weight, speed and jump height.

The levels keep shifting the rules of that one key. A gap that wants a sits right next to a wall that wants A, so you shift in mid-run. Caps Lock zones shift what Shift itself means. And shifting down in mid-air turns a fall into a glide. No other key would make sense for this game, and no other theme would either.
