lowercase / UPPERCASE

You are the missing letter. Every level is a sentence with one blank in it, and the world is built from that sentence: words are the floors, punctuation is the machinery, and a single yellow letter has to find its way into the gap.

Hold Shift and you become A: big, heavy and slow, strong enough to smash cracked words, slam down out of the air and push heavy words around. Let go and you are a again: small, light and fast, able to slip under low words, fly off springs and ride the wind. One key, two letters, and every obstacle asks the same question: which form, and when?

Two forms, one key

  • a (Shift released): runs fast and jumps high. Fits under low words, hangs and swings from commas, crosses hyphen bridges, drops through the holes between kerned letters, and flies twice as high off a spring. Floating words carry it up, it floats on ink, and a tailwind carries its jumps across gaps too wide for anyone.
  • A (Shift held): slow, with barely a hop, but heavy. Smashes cracked words, holds periods down to keep doors open, stomps enemies from above, and walks straight over kerning holes. It sinks floating words, pushes heavy words, sinks to the bottom of ink and walks straight into a headwind.

Grow in mid-air to slam: A dives straight down, breaks brick under it and knocks out the enemies beside where it lands. Growing needs headroom: under a low ceiling, Shift waits until there is room. Shrinking in mid-air keeps your speed, so a quick A-drop into a release turns into a long glide.

A world made of text

  • brick is a cracked wall. Only A breaks it.
  • lowlowlow stacked over the floor is a crawl space. Only a fits.
  • . is a pressure plate. Stand on it as A and its door opens.
  • -------- is a bridge that snaps under A.
  • ! is a spring, , is a hook to swing from, and letters set far apart (g a p) have holes a can fall through.
  • float rises while a stands on it and sinks under A. Left alone, it drifts back.
  • heavy only moves for A, and only a jumps high enough to climb onto it: push it to the wall, then climb.
  • ~~~ is ink. a floats up and jumps out; A sinks to the bottom and walks under walls a can't get past.
  • »» is wind. It blows a away, so walk into it as A; behind a, it carries a jump much further.
  • Words do what they say: ice slides, hot burns if you stay, up is a lift, sticky slows you down but lets you climb its walls.
  • LOCK zones turn on Caps Lock: you are A unless you hold Shift, until you find the key.
  • Hit a [*] power block from below to get asterisks to shoot.
  • Collect #, pass ; checkpoints, and finish by slotting into the blank: hide in a cr_ck.

Enemies

Anything red or orange bites, and touching it costs a life. Some die when A lands on them, some only to asterisks, and a few can't be stopped at all: you just have to get past.

  • & Walker: paces along its word. Stomp it as A or shoot it.
  • § Armour: asterisks bounce off it. Only a capital landing on it gets through.
  • ^ Spike: walks like the others, but landing on it hurts. Shoot it, or slam down beside it.
  • ! Hopper: hops on a beat. Pass under it while it is up.
  • ~ Flier: bobs and drifts through the air over gaps and bridges.
  • « < Arrow: fired from a launcher. Flies over a and hits A.
  • ¡ Riser: rises out of a hole in a spaced-out word, and stays down while you stand by it.
  • ' Drip: hangs under a ceiling and falls when you pass below. It grows back.
  • ° Spark: leaps out of a hot word and dives back in. Time your way past.
  • o···· Fire bar: a bar of fire turning around a hot o. a slips under it.
  • @ Curl: land on it as A and it curls into an o. Touch the o to kick it into other enemies.
  • ö Eye: wakes when you come near and drifts after you, even through words. Shoot it.
  • ↕ Case swapper: touch it and Shift works the other way until you touch another.
  • b Mimic: copies your case a moment late. Turn A just before you land on the small b.
  • | Cursor: chases you and erases the words behind you. Keep moving.

Enemies that walk sink in ink too, and patrol the bottom of the pool. A sinking onto one still squashes it.

The run

  • 15 levels in 5 chapters. The first two levels of each chapter teach something new; the third tests it and ends in a run that makes you switch faster and faster.
  • 60 seconds per level, three lives per run. Run out of time and the run is over.
  • Score for enemies, # collectibles and every second left on the clock, with your own top-10 table and a world high score list. Every 1500 points give back a lost life, never more than three.
  • Random mode: endless levels stitched together from hand-made sections, just for practice and fun (no high scores).
  • Two sound sets to choose from: Chip and Type.

World high scores

Every run from the first level goes to the world high scores, besides your own top 10. Type your name at the end (the last one you typed is offered again), then ← → switches between your table and the world's top 10 with your own place below it. The page also lists the fastest finish of every level. Only a name, the points and times per level, the platform and the date are sent; Options › Online scores turns it off.

Make your own levels

Editor in the menu opens My levels, where you build sentences of your own with the same pieces the chapters use.

  • Every piece and every enemy: type words straight onto the page and make them ice, hot, sticky or a hyphen bridge, then add bricks, springs, hooks, lifts, doors with their periods, floating and heavy words, ink pools, wind, Caps Lock zones, checkpoints, power blocks, # and all fifteen enemies.
  • Set the rules: the sentence, whether the blank wants a or A, the time limit and a ceiling. Drag the yellow squares to size lifts, floats, wind, ink, hooks and fliers.
  • Test as you go: P plays your level from the start, H from wherever the mouse is, and Esc brings you straight back. Warnings point at likely mistakes, Ctrl+Z undoes, and everything saves as you go.
  • Share it: reach the exit in a test play from the start and the level counts as finished. Share turns it into a code to paste anywhere, and Import turns a friend's code into a level of yours.

Shared levels

Put your finished levels on the shared levels page and play everyone else's. Paste a code to upload it, browse the list with a preview of every level drawn the way the game draws it, and copy any code into My levels › Import. The Shared levels button in My levels takes you there.

Controls

  • Move: ← → or A / D (left stick)
  • Jump: Space, ↑ or W (A / Cross)
  • Grow: hold Shift (either trigger)
  • Shoot: Ctrl, after hitting a power block (X / Square). In the browser, use F instead: Ctrl+W closes the tab.
  • Restart level: R  ·  Pause: Esc (Start)
  • Level editor: mouse and keyboard. Click to place and pick, drag to move, arrows or right drag to pan, P / H to test, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y to undo and redo. The game's How to play has a page on it.

Made for GitHub Game Off 2026: SHIFT

The theme is the controller. The whole game is played on the Shift key, and its only verb is the one the key was named for: on a typewriter, pressing Shift physically shifted the type into position to print the capital. Here it shifts a letter between its two cases, and with it everything that matters: size, weight, speed and jump height.

The levels keep shifting the rules of that one key. A gap that wants a sits right next to a wall that wants A, so you shift in mid-run. Caps Lock zones shift what Shift itself means. Shifting up in mid-air turns a jump into a slam, and shifting down turns a fall into a glide. No other key would make sense for this game, and no other theme would either.
