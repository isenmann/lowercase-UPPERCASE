Every moment in the game that could make a sound, each in four styles. Click a waveform to hear it, then Pick the one you want. You can mix styles, but one style for everything will sound most like one game. Your picks stay in this browser. When you're done, Copy picks and paste the list back to Claude.

All 152 sounds are synthesised from scratch by a script (oscillators, filtered noise, FM bells). No samples or third-party audio, so they are safe to commit to the public repo.

WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono. Loudness is pre-balanced: menu ticks and clock ticks are quiet, impacts and fanfares are loud. "Clock tick" and "Time up" would need a small hook that doesn't exist yet.
